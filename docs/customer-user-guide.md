# AR MULTIVENTURES — CUSTOMER USER GUIDE (WEB & MOBILE)

**Target Audience:** Corporate Customers, Procurement Officers, Project Engineers.

---

## 1. Getting Started
- **Web Portal:** Access `https://portal.armultiventures.com`
- **Mobile Companion:** Open **AR Multiventures** on your Android/iOS phone.
- **Login:** Enter your corporate work email and password.

---

## 2. Placing a New Granite Requisition (8-Step Wizard)
1. **Quarry Extraction Plant:** Select the certified extraction quarry closest to your site.
2. **Aggregate Material:** Choose from Granite 3/4" (20mm), 1/2" (12mm), 1" (25mm), Stone Base, Dust, or Boulders.
3. **Quantity:** Select or input order tonnage (Presets: 30T, 60T, 90T, 150T, 300T).
4. **Transportation Mode:** Choose *AR Multiventures Delivery*, *Self-Pickup at Quarry*, or *Haulage Only*.
5. **Truck Preference:** Choose preferred tipper capacity (e.g. 30T Heavy Tipper or 45T Articulated).
6. **Destination Site:** Select a pre-approved site or enter a new project site address and LGA.
7. **Schedule & Instructions:** Specify requested delivery date and offload bay notes.
8. **Live Price Breakdown:** Review pit-head aggregate total, haulage fee, loading charge, and discounts. Click **"Submit Requisition Now"**.

---

## 3. Invoices, Payments & Receipts
- **Online Checkout (Instant):** Tap **"Pay with Paystack"** to pay via Debit Card, Bank Transfer, or USSD with instant confirmation.
- **Direct Bank Transfer (NIP):** Transfer the exact invoice total to AR Multiventures official corporate accounts, then upload your bank deposit receipt and session ID.
- **Statements:** View running sub-ledger balance, credit limit, and official receipts under **Account**.

---

## 4. Operational Tracking & Proof of Delivery (POD)
- **Operational Tracking:** Monitor your order fulfillment progress (Tonnage Delivered vs. Ordered).
- **Scale Net Weight:** View the exact weighbridge certified tonnage before truck departs quarry.
- **Digital POD:** View site engineer signed PODs with offload photos directly in your order history.

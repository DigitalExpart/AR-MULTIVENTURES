# AR MULTIVENTURES — DRIVER MOBILE COMPANION USER GUIDE

**Target Audience:** Heavy Tipper Drivers, Logistics Operators.

---

## 1. Starting Your Shift
1. Open the **AR Multiventures** mobile app.
2. Sign in with your driver email (e.g. `driver.musa@armultiventures.com`).
3. Verify your assigned heavy tipper registration (e.g. `LSR-492-YY`) and active duty status (`🟢 ON ACTIVE DUTY`).

---

## 2. Executing a Delivery Mission Step-by-Step

### Step 1: Arrive at Quarry Gate
- When your truck pulls up to the quarry security dock, tap:
  **`🏭 1. CHECK IN AT QUARRY GATE`**

### Step 2: Scale & Depart Quarry
- Drive onto the weighbridge scale for tare weight, proceed to loading pit, and return to scale for gross weight.
- When scale ticket and physical waybill are received, tap:
  **`⚖️ 2. SCALED & DEPART QUARRY`**

### Step 3: Arrive at Construction Site
- Navigate to the destination site. Tap `📞 Call Site` if gate directions are needed.
- When truck pulls onto the customer offloading bay, tap:
  **`🏁 3. ARRIVED AT DELIVERY SITE`**

### Step 4: Digital Proof of Delivery (POD)
- Tap **`✍️ 4. CAPTURE DIGITAL POD`**.
- Input the receiving engineer's name, role, phone number, and delivered tonnage.
- Hand your phone to the site engineer to sign on the touchscreen pad.
- Take photos of the offloaded granite aggregate at the site.
- Tap **"Submit Digital POD"**.

---

## 3. Working Offline & Pending Sync
- **No Network at Site:** The app will automatically stage your signature and photos in your mobile outbox with status **`POD Staged Locally (Offline) — Pending Sync`**.
- **Reconnecting:** As soon as you drive into network coverage, tap **"Sync Now"** on the banner. The server will confirm the delivery and return your truck to available status.

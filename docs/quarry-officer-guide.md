# AR MULTIVENTURES — QUARRY LOADING & WEIGHBRIDGE OFFICER GUIDE

**Target Audience:** Quarry Gate Officers, Weighbridge Operators, Loading Supervisors.

---

## 1. Daily Operations Runbook

### Step 1: Truck Gate Check-In
1. Navigate to **Quarry Operations** $\rightarrow$ **Gate Queue**.
2. When an assigned truck arrives, verify the driver identity, truck registration, and scheduled requisition reference.
3. Click **"Check-In Truck"**. The truck enters the quarry loading queue.

### Step 2: Tare Weight Capture
1. Position the empty truck on the weighbridge platform.
2. Enter the tare weight in tonnes (e.g. `15.05 T`).
3. Click **"Record Tare Weight"**.

### Step 3: Loading Pit Session
1. Dispatch the truck to the aggregate loading bay.
2. Excavator / wheel loader fills the tipper with the requested granite size (e.g. Granite 3/4").
3. Update status to **"Loading Complete"**.

### Step 4: Gross Weight & Scale Ticket Issuance
1. Position the fully loaded truck on the weighbridge platform.
2. Enter the gross weight in tonnes (e.g. `45.10 T`).
3. The system automatically computes:
   $$\text{Net Weight} = \text{Gross Weight} - \text{Tare Weight} = 45.10 - 15.05 = 30.05\text{ T}$$
4. Review tolerance variance against planned requisition tonnage.
5. Click **"Issue Electronic Scale Ticket"**. The truck is cleared for dispatch.
